## 👋 About Me

<p align="justify">
Hey — I'm <strong>Rafiul Islam</strong>, an <strong>IoT &amp; Robotics Engineering</strong> undergrad at the <strong>University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB)</strong>, and a builder at heart. I spend my days stitching together microcontrollers, sensors and ML models into systems that actually <em>do</em> something useful in the physical world — from a classroom-attention monitor that lights up a teacher's desk, to a bike that refuses to start without its rightful RFID card.
</p>

<p align="justify">

</p>
