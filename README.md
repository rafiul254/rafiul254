## 🤖 About Me

> *"Code the mind. Build the circuit. Connect the world."*

<img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/13HgwGsXF0aiGY/giphy.gif" width="270" alt="Coding Animation"/>

Hey! I'm **Rafiul** — an **IoT & Robotics Engineering student** at UFTB, Bangladesh 🇧🇩, currently working as an **ML Engineering Intern at FlyRank AI**.

I don't sit on just one layer of the stack. I work from **flashing firmware on an ESP32**, to designing **REST APIs**, to training **ML models**, to shipping real products. If it involves hardware talking to software talking to AI — that's my domain.

When I step away from the lab, I'm exploring new places ✈️, lost in a good book 📚, shooting photos 📸, or editing videos 🎬.
