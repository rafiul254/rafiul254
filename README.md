## 🤖 About Me

&gt; *"Facilitating knowledge sharing, embracing diverse experiences, and optimizing solutions for collective success."*

Hey there! I'm **Rafiul Islam** — an **IoT & Robotics Engineer** from Bangladesh 🇧🇩 who loves building things that bridge the physical and digital worlds. I spend my days tinkering with microcontrollers, training neural networks, and writing code that makes hardware come alive.

- 🎓 **B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering** from International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC)
- 🌱 Currently exploring **Advanced IoT Architectures**, **Robotics Systems**, **Computer Vision**, and **Scalable AI/ML Solutions**
- 🏆 Passionate about **Competitive Programming** — always chasing that optimal solution
- 🌍 **Travel enthusiast** — I believe the best ideas come from new experiences
- 📚 **Bookworm** — halfway through a story is my happy place
- 🎬 **Movie buff** — CineMatch AI is literally built from my love for films
- ☕ **Fuel**: Coffee, and the dream that one day AI will stop hallucinating APIs that don't exist 😅
