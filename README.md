### 🕷️ *"With great power comes great responsibility"*

Hey there, I'm **Rafiul Islam** — an **IoT & Robotics Engineering** undergrad at the **University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh (UFTB)**, and a builder at heart. I spend my days stitching together microcontrollers, sensors, and ML models into systems that actually *do* something useful in the physical world — from a classroom-attention monitor that lights up a teacher's desk, to a bike that won't start without its rightful RFID card.

I treat every project like Peter Parker treats New York: **show up, scope out the problem, ship a fix, and leave the city (codebase) cleaner than I found it.** That means clean layered architecture, sane abstractions, sensible `README.md`s, and hardware-software contracts that don't fall apart the moment someone breathes on them.

When I'm not wiring up an ESP32 or training a model, I'm usually reading, traveling, or chasing down the next framework that promises to make me 10× faster. Sometimes it even does. 🕸️
